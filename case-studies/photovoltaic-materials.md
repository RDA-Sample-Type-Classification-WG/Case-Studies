# Samples in the photovoltaic materials community

## About the photovoltaic materials community

We are a scientific institute focusing on the development of
photovoltaic materials, specifically on the area of perovskite-based
semiconductors.

## Samples in the photovoltaic materials community

We prepare thin-film devices, mostly prepared on glass substrates of
area 25x25 mm² and 1 mm thickness.  Sizes can differ to up to 150x150
mm² and substrates could also vary on material, to metallic- to
polymer-based substrates.

## Characterising properties

* Efficiency (PCE),
* Stability (through MPPT),
* Band gap through different methods (PL, UPS, EQE, etc),
* Stack sequence,
* Processing methods for each layer.
