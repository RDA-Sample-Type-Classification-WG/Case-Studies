# Samples in the Australian isotopes community

## Samples in the Australian isotopes community

Samples collected for the purpose of isotope analysis.  These can
originate from soil, plant, water, or animal material.  The sample may
also be a chemical compound contained within the original sample
material.

## Characterising properties

The measurement and analytical methods properties distinguishes stable
isotope samples from other samples.  These are the properties that can
create a unique sample that is comparable both to other stable isotope
samples and to similar sample matrices with other measurement
properties.
