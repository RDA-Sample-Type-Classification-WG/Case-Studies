# Samples in the Geochemistry community

## Samples in the Geochemistry community

Samples in geochemical community are generally rocks and minerals.
Within these, different rocks and minerals may be sorted into
(generally hierarchical) individual families and species following
longstanding lithology and minerology definitions.

In some cases sample types may extend to extraterrestrial materials,
soils/regolith, fluids such as groundwater, fluid inclusions, or
synthetic materials such as glasses and products of experimental
petrology.

## Characterising properties

Sample materials are differentiated into individual families and
species following longstanding lithology and minerology definitions.

Samples are also often distinguished by the sampling method used to
collect them (e.g. sampled directly from outcrop, drill-holes,
submarine sampling).

Note it is also common to have multiple 'child' samples from sample
preparation, from crushing and physical separation, to wet chemistry
products.

Sampling scale should also be considered in some cases, i.e. what does
the data reflect, a whole rock or unit, entire mineral, or a zone
within that rock or mineral?
